It was kinda adventure. The route itself should've been only 200km long (similar to our [[Bicycle Trip. Moscow -> Tver. 200 km|first]] trip). But we decided to risk and ride on highway road (which is forbidden to ride on bicycle). And we were pulled over and couldn't ride highway anymore.

At first glance it wasn't much of a problem. However, later it turned out that highway is the only road out there and since we didn't want to ride it again, we decided to ride through fields and very bad roads. There were some places where we couldn't ride and we walked through the tall grass.

Even though it was not that convenient, I think it was kinda cool and I like this experience after all. I would not bother if something like this will happen again and we need to ride through fields.
