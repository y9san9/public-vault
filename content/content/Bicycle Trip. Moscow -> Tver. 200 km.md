This was in my first season of riding a bike. Me and my friend Matthew just spontaneously decided to ride from one city to another and have chosen Tver to be our target. I can't say I remember a lot of things, but we did it in 12 hours or so. And we were very tired. Something about 4k calories lost even though we ate a lot of fast-food on the way.

No preparation at all. No special food. No training in advance. The most fun part was me riding not on a proper bicycle. I was riding Mountain Bike (Merida). It's cheap, completely not suitable for such rides. But I'll have one more time riding this bicycle to the other city. And the [[Bicycle Trip. Moscow -> Tula. 350 km|next time]] trip was much easier in terms of physics.

But this was definitely a fun journey! We live for that kind of stuff.
